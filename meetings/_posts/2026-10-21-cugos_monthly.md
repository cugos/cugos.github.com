[Toby Champion](https://tobychampion.com/) will talk about going beyond GIS layers to build a spatiotemporal knowledge graph of Bainbridge Island. [Let’s Map Bainbridge](https://letsmapbainbridge.org/) is a volunteer-led project to help people understand Bainbridge Island as it is now and how it came to be that way. A road end today was once a steamboat landing, connecting it to vessels, routes, communities and people from the island’s past. Behind LMB is **CityGraph**, a platform Toby is building to integrate data from OpenStreetMap, OpenHistoricalMap, government GIS, museum collections, historical records and local research into a single geographic knowledge graph. Everything gets a unique URL in a common identity space, with authority control, provenance and a shared controlled vocabulary drawing on sources including Getty AAT and Wikidata. Technically, it’s PostGIS, QGIS, RDF, GeoSPARQL and CIDOC CRM, with Claude Code using MCP to work across the codebase, query the research/knowledge graph, and directly control QGIS.
